
# Tim Beaudet's Indie Adventure: _Generative-AI Policy_

## The Rules
1. Remember the human.
2. Do not _PUSH_ Generative-AI.
3. No doomsaying.
4. No hypebole.

## Not Anti-AI and Not Pro-AI

Generative-AI is here to stay whether you like it or not. Pandora's box has already been opened.  
I will **not** be generating my code, art, audio or content for my games.  
I will use it where I feel it fits for me, see Usage.  
Use Generative-AI if you want to, don't use if if you don't want to. **Don't push others.**

## My Usage of Generative-AI

* Searching / Googling.
* Brainstorming
* Translating Error Messages (like C++ template/linker non-sense)
* Code-review; identify issues, areas to simply, or optimize. May allow it to suggest but not make the changes.

Will **not** be using generated code, art, audio or content in my games.

## Why Don't You Use Generative-AI more?
* It magnifies the worst parts of coding (for me).
* It minimizes the best, good/fun, parts of coding (for me).
* It is not _satisfying._ Does not feel like _mine._
* It started/makes me a lazy developer. It makes me not want to try solving a problem, just reach for "do it for me."
* I simply do not want to use it more.

## Wouldn't Generative-AI be More Productive?
Possibly? But at what cost and is moving faster even a goal worth pursuing? Does moving faster create more value in the world?  
I want to create experiences to share with others. I want to help others create the visions they have in their head. I want my work and effort to bring value to others and feel satisfying and as if "I" did/made it.

## My Other Thoughts
* I don't care what Generative-AI _CAN_ do.
* I don't care what Generative-AI _CAN'T_ do.
